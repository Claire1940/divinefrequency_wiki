<Section icon={Target} title="Best Mod Priorities by Combat Archetype">

The three practical archetypes are firearms-focused, psionics-focused, and hybrid. None should be treated as a permanent ranking because the best choice depends on your available materials, preferred range, and the encounters ahead. Instead, use each archetype as a framework for deciding which modifications deserve priority.

| Archetype | First Priority | Second Priority | Recommended Pairing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-Focused | Damage consistency and handling | Accuracy or ammunition efficiency | A psionic utility option |
| Psionics-Focused | Effect strength and reliable recovery | Duration, range, or resource efficiency | A dependable firearm |
| Hybrid | Reliability across both systems | A focused answer to one weakness | One primary weapon and one flexible ability |
| Experimental | Low-cost testing | Reversible or broadly useful effects | Existing equipment with spare resources |

### Firearms-Focused Setup

A firearm-centered build should make standard engagements more predictable. Start with upgrades that improve the weapon’s regular performance before chasing specialized effects. A weapon that is easier to control and more efficient with ammunition can remain useful across several encounter types.

Use a psionic option as support rather than dividing your entire progression plan. Crowd control, emergency utility, or a tool for close-range pressure can protect the firearm investment without requiring equal spending in both systems.

### Psionics-Focused Setup

A psionics-focused setup benefits from deliberate timing. The strongest ability is not always the best ability to use first, particularly when a smaller effect can resolve a minor encounter. Prioritize modifications that improve the situations where your abilities already perform well.

A firearm remains important for sustained damage when psionic resources are unavailable or better saved. This secondary weapon does not need to compete with the primary system; it only needs to cover predictable gaps.

### Hybrid Setup

Hybrid builds are flexible but can become unfocused. Select one primary damage method first, then use the second system to solve a specific weakness. For example, a firearm may provide sustained ranged pressure while a psionic option handles control or emergency defense.

Avoid spreading upgrade materials equally across every available category. A hybrid setup still needs a clear center of gravity.

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Unfocused Spending">
Do not purchase a modification simply because it is available. If it does not improve your primary combat plan or cover a known weakness, save the materials for a more useful upgrade.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Resource Management and Upgrade Safety">

Modification decisions compete with ammunition, healing, psionic resources, and future progression. The safest approach is to treat these supplies as one shared survival budget. Spending everything on a single improvement can leave the loadout powerful in theory but fragile during exploration.

Before upgrading, check whether you have enough resources to handle the next required encounter and whether the modification supports your current progression path. The game’s Early Access content currently covers two of three planned chapters, so future updates may also change how valuable a particular setup feels.

| Resource | Protect It For | Spend It When | Common Mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ammunition | Required fights and sustained encounters | The target is dangerous or difficult to bypass | Clearing every minor threat with premium ammo |
| Healing | Failed escapes and high-pressure rooms | Recovery is necessary to continue safely | Healing immediately after minor damage |
| Psionic Resources | Control, utility, and dangerous targets | The ability solves a clear combat problem | Using high-impact effects on weak enemies |
| Upgrade Materials | Reliable or complementary improvements | The benefit supports repeated situations | Splitting materials across too many options |
| Exploration Time | Optional rooms and hidden routes | The area is safe enough to investigate | Repeating the same blocked path without notes |

### When to Delay a Modification

Delay an upgrade when:

- You have not yet identified your preferred combat range.
- The upgrade would consume resources needed for a known progression blocker.
- The effect is highly specialized but current encounters remain varied.
- You are still testing whether firearms or psionic tools better fit your play style.
- A major update has recently changed balance or progression behavior.

### When to Commit

Commit when:

- The same weakness has appeared across multiple encounters.
- The modification improves your main tool without exhausting your reserve.
- You have a dependable backup option.
- The upgrade supports both exploration and required combat.
- You can explain the role of the upgrade in one sentence.

### Controlled Ranged

This setup favors distance, readable engagements, and careful target selection. Improve the firearm’s consistency first, then add a psionic option that helps when enemies close the gap. The goal is not maximum burst damage; it is maintaining control without wasting ammunition.

### Ability Pressure

This setup uses psionic tools to shape the encounter. Save powerful effects for groups, dangerous targets, or situations where normal weapons are inefficient. A firearm should remain available for periods when ability resources are recovering.

### Balanced Explorer

A balanced explorer carries one reliable firearm, one frequently useful psionic option, and modifications that support the same range or encounter plan. This is a strong starting point for players who are still learning how the game’s areas and enemies interact.

### Resource Saver

A resource-saving build prioritizes efficiency and consistency. It may deal less burst damage than a specialized setup, but it reduces the chance of entering an optional area without enough supplies. This approach is especially useful when you are mapping routes and recording blocked paths.
