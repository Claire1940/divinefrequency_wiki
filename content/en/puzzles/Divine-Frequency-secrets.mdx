<Section icon={Search} title="Read Environmental Clues Before Using Hints">

Puzzle solving in Divine Frequency should begin with the environment itself. Inspect unusual markings, repeated symbols, lighting changes, object placement, and rooms that appear intentionally framed. These details are more useful when compared with nearby spaces rather than viewed in isolation.

Use a progressive hint system to protect the discovery experience. First describe what you noticed, then identify the relationship between clues, and only afterward apply the interaction order. This keeps the solution useful without immediately removing the mystery.

| Investigation Stage | Question to Ask | Typical Result |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | What looks unusual in this room? | You identify a unique object, symbol, or lighting change |
| Comparison | Which details repeat nearby? | You separate instructions from background decoration |
| Connection | What object or mechanism matches the clue? | You locate the likely interaction point |
| Sequence | Is there an order, direction, or pattern? | You determine the actions required |
| Verification | Did the environment respond? | You confirm the puzzle or return for another clue |

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Inspect the Full Room">
    Before interacting with anything, scan the walls, floor, ceiling, lighting, objects, and nearby exits. Check whether the room contains a detail that appears isolated or unusually emphasized.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Compare Repeated Motifs">
    Look for symbols, markings, colors, sounds, or object arrangements that appear more than once. Repetition may establish a sequence, while a single unusual detail may identify the correct target.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Trace the Clue to an Interaction">
    Connect the environmental detail to a door, mechanism, object, or restricted route. If the relationship is unclear, search adjacent rooms before spending resources.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Apply the Smallest Possible Action">
    Test the most direct interaction first. Avoid activating multiple unrelated objects when the puzzle has not yet revealed a clear order.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Confirm the Result">
    Check for a changed light, sound, door, object state, or newly accessible path. If nothing changes, return to the comparison stage instead of assuming the puzzle is inactive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Hidden Areas and Optional Discoveries">

Hidden areas are often easiest to find after completing the immediate objective in a location. The main route gives you landmarks, clears uncertainty, and may change the environment. Once the required step is complete, retrace the area and check side rooms, blocked sightlines, unusual wall sections, and routes that previously appeared inactive.

Do not assume that every optional space contains a combat encounter. Some discoveries may provide resources, lore details, or context for the setting. Search the area after a major interaction because rewards and clues can be placed away from the point where the secret was activated.

| Area Signal | First Check | Follow-Up Action |
|---|---|---|
| Unusual wall section | Inspect nearby edges and lighting | Return after the local objective advances |
| Empty or isolated room | Search for an interaction or visual motif | Check adjacent routes for a concealed entrance |
| Blocked path | Record its location and apparent requirement | Revisit after story progress or new access |
| Repeated symbol | Compare it with surrounding rooms | Test the matching mechanism in a logical order |
| New environmental change | Re-scan the completed route | Search for rewards near the changed area |

A useful exploration loop is:

- Enter a new area and identify the main route.
- Check obvious side rooms without spending rare resources.
- Complete the nearby essential objective.
- Revisit unusual spaces and previously blocked branches.
- Search the interaction area for rewards, lore, or a second clue.
- Record anything that still appears inaccessible.

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Unnecessary Backtracking">
Do not repeatedly search an entire chapter without a reason. Keep a short list of blocked doors, suspicious rooms, and unresolved symbols, then revisit those locations after meaningful progression.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Prepare for Secret Encounters">

Some optional discoveries can lead to high-pressure encounters involving limited space, environmental hazards, or repeated waves of danger. These events deserve different preparation from a normal exploration fight. Inspect the surrounding area first, identify a retreat route, and decide which resources are worth preserving for the final phase.

The safest loadout is usually a focused combination rather than a collection of partially upgraded tools. Carry one reliable firearm or damage source, one flexible psionic option, and enough healing to recover from an unexpected mistake. A hybrid setup is especially useful when the encounter range is unknown.

| Preparation Area | Recommended Priority | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Healing | Enter with a useful reserve | Mistakes are more costly in confined spaces |
| Ammunition | Preserve reliable damage | Optional fights may last longer than expected |
| Psionic resources | Save high-impact abilities | Control or burst effects can change the final phase |
| Escape route | Identify before activation | Retreating through unknown rooms increases risk |
| Inventory space | Clear unnecessary items | Secret encounters may provide rewards afterward |

  <Card title="Resource Discipline">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Safe Retreat">

  </Card>

<Callout type="tip" title="Combat Rule">
