<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat and Build Preparation for Story Routes">

Some story routes and optional discoveries may place the player in confined spaces or unfamiliar combat situations. A focused build is more reliable than spreading upgrades across every available option during the opening content.

Firearms provide consistent ranged pressure, while psionic options can offer utility, control, or a response to unusual encounters. A hybrid setup is useful when exploration repeatedly changes the distance and threat level of combat.

| Build Approach | Main Strength | Main Risk | Early Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-focused | Consistent ranged damage and readable resource use | Ammunition dependence and weaker flexibility | Improve damage consistency, handling, and accuracy |
| Psionics-focused | Flexible utility, control, and burst options | Ability timing and resource availability | Keep a dependable firearm for sustained encounters |
| Hybrid | Coverage across multiple ranges and situations | Upgrade materials may become spread too thin | Choose one primary damage method first |
| Exploration-focused | Better preparation for mixed encounters and optional routes | May delay specialized damage upgrades | Improve reliability, recovery, and emergency options |

For most players, the safest early plan is to choose one dependable damage source, then use the second system to address its weakness. Do not spend every high-impact ability on isolated enemies if an unexplored route or difficult objective may follow.

| Encounter Situation | Recommended Response | Resource Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Isolated hostile | Use the most efficient reliable option | Avoid rare resources when retreat is possible |
| Fast pursuer | Break line of sight and use obstacles | Keep emergency healing available |
| Confined room | Identify exits before engaging | Do not trigger optional events without preparation |
| Mixed pressure | Combine firearm damage with utility abilities | Preserve one response for the most dangerous target |
| Story-critical encounter | Enter with a clear loadout and route | Refill or upgrade before committing when possible |
