<Section icon={Swords} title="Opening Combat and Resource Priorities">

The opening areas teach the central rhythm of Divine Frequency: observe the encounter, choose the safest effective tool, and avoid spending everything during the first exchange. Firearms provide dependable ranged pressure, while psionic options can offer flexible damage, control, utility, or emergency responses.

Do not commit to a highly specialized build too early. A focused setup is easier to manage while you are still learning enemy behavior, resource availability, and the shape of each area.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Check the room before engaging">
    Look for cover, exits, narrow routes, and nearby doors before activating suspicious objects or entering an unfamiliar space. Knowing where to retreat is often more valuable than opening with maximum damage.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Test your available combat tools">
    Compare a reliable firearm with at least one psionic option during safe encounters. Learn which tool works best at range, against pressure, or when ammunition must be conserved.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Control the distance">
    Create space before attacking again, especially against close-range enemies or fast pursuers. Corners, doorways, and previously cleared rooms can help prevent being trapped.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reserve high-impact resources">
    Keep powerful abilities, healing, and specialized ammunition for groups, difficult targets, blocked routes, or encounters that cannot be avoided.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Review your setup after major progress">
    When a new area introduces different ranges or encounter pressures, adjust your loadout. Improve reliability first, then add specialization that addresses the problem you are actually facing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Combat Tool | Best Role | Main Limitation | Early Upgrade Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms | Consistent ranged damage | Ammunition management | Accuracy, handling, damage consistency |
| Psionic options | Utility, control, or burst responses | Ability availability and recovery | Effect strength, duration, efficiency |
| Weapon modifications | Specialization | Upgrade materials and capacity | Bonuses that support one clear combat plan |
| Hybrid loadout | Coverage across varied encounters | Slower specialization | Reliability before situational bonuses |

<Callout type="warning" title="Resource Warning">
Avoid treating every room as a full-clear encounter. If an isolated enemy does not block progression, compare the reward with the ammunition, healing, and time required to defeat it.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Puzzles, Secrets, and Backtracking">

Environmental clues are an important part of the Divine Frequency experience. Repeated symbols, unusual object placement, sound changes, lighting, and deliberately framed spaces may indicate that an area deserves a second look.

Use a progressive hint method. First inspect the environment, then compare repeated details, and only afterward test the likely interaction. This reduces wasted resources and preserves the atmosphere of the game.

| Discovery Type | First Observation | Follow-Up Action | Spoiler Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Environmental puzzle | Notice repeated symbols or a unique visual detail | Compare nearby rooms and identify a sequence | Low |
| Hidden area | Check unusual walls, side rooms, and blocked sightlines | Return after a nearby objective changes access | Medium |
| Optional encounter | Watch for unusual sound, restricted movement, or empty space | Prepare healing and identify a retreat route | High |
| Lore discovery | Examine objects and locations that appear deliberately emphasized | Revisit after story interactions | Medium |

A strong backtracking routine has four parts:

- Finish the immediate required objective.
- Mark the location with a recognizable landmark.
- Return through cleared spaces rather than unknown rooms.
- Search for environmental changes before using resources or triggering an encounter.

<Callout type="tip" title="Puzzle Tip">
If a mechanism seems inactive, do not assume it is broken. Check whether a nearby interaction, story event, or traversal change must occur first.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Build Planning and Playthrough Checklist">

A reliable first build should solve ordinary encounters consistently rather than maximize one narrow damage pattern. Players who prefer direct combat can focus on firearms, while players who enjoy active abilities may favor psionic tools. A hybrid setup is useful when exploration frequently changes engagement range, but it requires stricter upgrade discipline.

| Archetype | Strength | Weakness | Beginner Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-focused | Reliable ranged pressure | Ammunition dependence | Improve consistency and keep one utility option |
| Psionics-focused | Flexible control and burst tools | Limited ability availability | Save major effects for dangerous encounters |
| Hybrid | Adaptable across ranges | Slower specialization | Choose one primary damage source first |
| Balanced progression | Easier resource decisions | Less immediate specialization | Upgrade reliability before experimentation |

Use this priority order during a first playthrough:

1. Improve the tool you use most often.
2. Keep one emergency response for close pressure or difficult encounters.
3. Add utility that solves a known weakness.
4. Avoid spreading upgrade materials across unrelated options.
5. Reevaluate after entering a new Act or discovering a different combat demand.
