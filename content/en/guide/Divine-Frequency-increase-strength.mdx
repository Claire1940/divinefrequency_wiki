### Firearms-Focused Strength

A firearms-focused build should not mean carrying every weapon equally. Select one dependable weapon role and improve the qualities that make it easier to use under pressure. Accuracy, handling, and ammunition efficiency can be more valuable than a narrow damage increase if missed shots or poor positioning are consuming your resources.

Keep a psionic option for situations where enemies collapse distance, the environment limits sightlines, or conventional ammunition should be preserved. This setup is especially useful for players who prefer to identify threats, establish distance, and control the pace of an encounter.

### Psionics-Focused Strength

A psionics-focused build gains power from timing rather than constant ability use. Save high-impact effects for groups, dangerous targets, blocked routes, or encounters with limited space. Using every ability against minor threats can leave the build less effective when the encounter becomes serious.

A firearm remains valuable even when psionics are the main focus. It provides a fallback during recovery windows and reduces pressure on your ability resources. The strongest version of this build is usually selective, not wasteful.

### Hybrid Strength

Hybrid builds are useful when the area combines exploration, mixed enemy pressure, and changing engagement distances. Their weakness is diluted investment. To avoid this, select one primary system and treat the second as support until the main plan is stable.

| Build Path | Primary Strength | Secondary Need | Upgrade Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-focused | Ranged consistency | Close-range or utility answer | Handling, accuracy, efficiency |
| Psionics-focused | Flexible effects | Sustained damage fallback | Effect strength, recovery, duration |
| Hybrid | Broad encounter coverage | Clear spending priority | Reliability before specialization |

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Split Investment">
Do not upgrade several weapons and abilities at the same pace unless you have a specific reason. Dividing materials too early can make every option feel incomplete.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Resource Management That Preserves Strength">

A powerful build can still feel weak when its resources are poorly managed. Divine Frequency’s opening areas reward careful exploration, deliberate combat, and a consistent route through branching spaces. Treat ammunition, healing, upgrade materials, and psionic resources as one shared survival budget.

Before entering an unfamiliar room, identify nearby cover and a possible retreat path. This habit improves survivability without requiring a specific item or exact numerical build. It also prevents a single surprise encounter from forcing you to spend every high-value resource.

| Resource | Spend When | Preserve When | Common Mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ammunition | A target blocks safe progress | The area offers a retreat route | Firing without controlling distance |
| Healing | Damage threatens the next objective | Minor damage can be managed safely | Entering the next room already depleted |
| Psionic resources | Control or burst prevents major risk | The encounter is isolated and predictable | Using abilities on every minor target |
| Upgrade materials | The improvement supports your main plan | You have not identified your preferred role | Buying unrelated upgrades immediately |

Use the following habits to increase practical strength:

- Explore side rooms before advancing through a major objective area.
- Remember locked doors, blocked paths, and containers that require later access.
- Keep one reliable damage option available when testing a new ability or modification.
- Save high-impact tools for groups, dangerous targets, and special-preparation encounters.
- Revisit unresolved paths after major story progress instead of forcing them immediately.
- Test one build change at a time so you can identify what actually improved performance.

The current Early Access version is designed to evolve. The [Steam Community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2548880/) is useful for checking discussions, announcements, and player reports before assuming that an unusual interaction is a permanent mechanic or a progression requirement.
