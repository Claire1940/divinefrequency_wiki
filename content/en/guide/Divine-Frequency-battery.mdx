<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step Resource Route">

The safest way to handle a possible battery-related resource is to follow a consistent exploration routine. This method works whether the final game uses batteries as a named item or whether players are using the term to describe power, ammunition, or another limited supply.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Inspect the Area Before Spending">
    Enter unfamiliar rooms slowly and identify exits, cover, side paths, locked access points, and interactable objects. Do not immediately use ammunition, healing, or psionic abilities simply because a resource is available. First determine whether the room contains a required objective, an optional encounter, or a route that can be postponed.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Establish a Safe Return Route">
    Keep track of cleared rooms and recognizable landmarks. A safe route gives you room to retreat when a fast pursuer or close-range hostile creates pressure. It also prevents a resource mistake from forcing a complete restart of the surrounding exploration.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Use the Cheapest Reliable Tool">
    Choose the weapon or ability that solves the immediate threat without consuming a rare reserve. Firearms provide dependable ranged damage, while psionic options can offer control, utility, unusual access, or burst damage. Do not use the strongest option against every isolated enemy.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Protect the Emergency Reserve">
    Keep enough ammunition, healing, and ability resources for an unavoidable fight or progression blocker. A reserve is especially important before activating suspicious objects, entering restricted areas, or following a route with limited escape space.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Record Unresolved Paths">
    Mark doors, containers, side rooms, and environmental mechanisms that cannot yet be completed. Return after a story objective, new ability, or traversal change instead of repeatedly spending resources on the same blocked route.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Situation | First response | Resource priority |
|---|---|---|
| Isolated early enemy | Create distance and use a reliable basic attack | Low-cost damage |
| Fast pursuer | Break line of sight and retreat through cleared space | Escape tools, then healing |
| Unknown room | Identify exits and cover before engaging | Information first |
| Optional encounter | Inspect the arena and prepare a retreat | Full reserve preferred |
| Blocked route | Record the location and continue the objective | Do not force progress |

<Callout type="tip" title="Route Planning Tip">

<Section icon={Swords} title="Battery-Friendly Combat and Builds">

Divine Frequency supports more than one combat approach. A firearms-focused setup emphasizes reliable ranged damage, a psionics-focused setup emphasizes flexible abilities and utility, and a hybrid setup balances both systems. For early exploration, the hybrid approach is often the most forgiving because it reduces dependence on one resource type.

Do not interpret “battery-friendly” as a confirmed in-game build label. Here, it means a setup that avoids waste and maintains options when ammunition, healing, or psionic resources become limited.

| Archetype | Main strength | Main risk | Battery-friendly adjustment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-focused | Consistent ranged damage | Ammunition dependence | Improve accuracy, handling, and efficiency before narrow specialization |
| Psionics-focused | Control, utility, and flexible responses | Ability resource limits | Save high-impact effects for groups or dangerous targets |
| Hybrid | Coverage across ranges and resource types | Slower specialization | Choose one primary damage method and use the other for support |
| Defensive plan | Better survival during exploration | Lower immediate damage | Preserve healing by improving positioning and escape options |

A battery-conscious loadout should answer three questions before each major area:

- Can the primary tool handle ordinary enemies without excessive spending?
- What option remains if the preferred resource runs low?
- Is enough healing or emergency utility available for an unexpected encounter?

### Handling a Suspected Battery Bug

Early Access builds can change between updates. If a battery-like object appears inaccessible, fails to activate, or behaves differently after a patch, first separate a technical issue from a progression requirement.

Use this order:

1. Confirm that the current Steam build has finished updating.
2. Recheck the objective state and nearby interaction points.
3. Return to the area after completing the immediate story requirement.
4. Test the interaction from a different position or approach.
5. Review official Steam announcements and community discussions for a current fix.
