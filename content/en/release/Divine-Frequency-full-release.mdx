<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat, Builds, and Exploration Priorities">

Divine Frequency supports several broad play styles. The available reference material describes firearms-focused, psionics-focused, and hybrid approaches. None should be treated as a permanent ranking because the game remains in development and the ideal choice depends on encounter design, available resources, and personal comfort.

| Archetype | Main Strength | Main Risk | Best Starting Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms-focused | Consistent ranged damage | Ammunition dependence | Improve handling, accuracy, and damage consistency |
| Psionics-focused | Flexible damage and utility | Ability resource limits | Save high-impact effects for dangerous encounters |
| Hybrid | Broad encounter coverage | Slower specialization | Choose one primary system and use the other for support |
| Balanced progression | Stable early development | Fewer specialized bonuses | Upgrade reliability before narrow bonuses |

For most first-time players, a hybrid foundation is practical: maintain one dependable firearm, keep one flexible psionic option available, and select modifications that reinforce the same combat plan. This does not require equal investment in every system. It means using the second system to cover the primary system’s most important weakness.

A firearms-focused setup works well when maintaining distance is comfortable. A psionics-focused setup offers more active utility but requires better timing and resource awareness. A hybrid setup is adaptable, although it may reach peak specialization later.

<Callout type="info" title="Build Advice">
Upgrade consistency before specialization. Reliable damage, manageable resource costs, and a dependable emergency option are usually more valuable during the opening content than several narrow bonuses.
</Callout>

For exploration, use the same principle: finish the essential objective first, then revisit optional areas when your inventory and equipment can support the risk. Areas with unusual sound design, repeated symbols, conspicuous empty space, or environmental changes may deserve a second look, especially after a nearby story interaction.
