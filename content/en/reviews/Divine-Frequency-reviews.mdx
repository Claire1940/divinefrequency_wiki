<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat, Atmosphere, and RPG Systems">

The core of most positive impressions centers on three pillars: the retro-styled gunplay, the oppressive atmosphere, and the RPG layer that differentiates it from pure boomer shooters.

**What players generally highlight:**

- **Shooting feel**: Fast, readable encounters that reward positioning and distance control rather than raw reflexes alone.
- **Surreal horror tone**: Reality-bending environments, analog-horror visual language, and sound design that keeps tension high without relying on jump scares alone.
- **RPG progression**: Character upgrades and psionic-flavored abilities give combat more depth than a typical retro shooter.
- **Exploration density**: Branching paths, locked doors, and optional encounters reward methodical play.

**Where the Early Access build draws criticism:**

- Only **two of three chapters** are playable, so the story stops mid-arc.
- English-only support limits accessibility for non-English players.
- As with most small-team Early Access launches, expect rough edges in performance and encounter balance during the opening weeks.
