<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat Fundamentals for New Players">

The opening hours reward careful players. Rushing into unfamiliar rooms, dumping ammunition into the first enemy you see, and ignoring cover are the fastest ways to die in Divine Frequency horror. The game supports both firearms and psionic options, and understanding when each one matters is the foundation of every good build.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Firearms">
    - **Reliable ranged damage**
    - Best for open engagements and controlled elimination
    - Limited by ammunition supply
    - Upgrade for accuracy and consistency
  </Card>
  <Card title="Psionic Options">
    - **Flexible damage and utility**
    - Crowd control and burst potential
    - Independent of conventional ammo
    - Limited by ability availability and recovery
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid Loadout">
    - **Balanced encounter coverage**
    - One dependable firearm plus one psionic tool
    - Reduces single-resource dependence
    - Requires disciplined upgrade spending
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

| Combat Category | Best Use | Main Resource | Upgrade Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firearms | Open fights, keeping distance | Ammunition | Damage consistency, handling, ammo efficiency |
| Psionic Options | Crowd control, burst, utility | Ability resources | Effect strength, recovery, duration |
| Modifications | Build specialization | Upgrade materials | Complementary bonuses, not stacked effects |
| Hybrid | Mixed-pressure exploration | Shared survival budget | Reliability first, specialization second |

<Callout type="tip" title="Combat Rhythm Tip">
Before entering any unfamiliar room, identify your exits and nearby cover. Preserve high-impact abilities for groups and blocked routes; do not burn them on isolated threats.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="First Hours: Step-by-Step Survival Plan">

Follow this sequence to establish a reliable routine in the opening acts. Each step builds on the previous one, so resist the urge to skip ahead to optional encounters before your resources can support the risk.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Learn the combat rhythm">
    Use cover, control engagement distance, and test both firearm and psionic options early so you know which tool works at which range. Spend the safest available option when learning a new enemy encounter.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Explore methodically">
    Check side rooms before committing to major objectives. Note every locked door, blocked route, and unopenable container as a future checkpoint rather than forcing progress now.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Manage your survival budget">
    Treat ammunition, healing supplies, upgrade materials, and psionic resources as one budget. Spend when an upgrade or item solves a clear problem, not simply because you acquired it.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Build a simple progression plan">
    Choose one reliable damage approach first, then add complementary modifications. A focused early setup outperforms several half-developed options.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Separate required from optional">
    Distinguish story-critical progression from optional discoveries. Early Access content may change, so keep a short list of unresolved locations to revisit after major story advances.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Checkpoint Habit">
Before leaving any major area, confirm three things: the main objective advanced, important resources were collected, and blocked paths were recorded. This single habit eliminates most frustrating backtracking.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Enemy Threats and How to Handle Them">

Divine Frequency horror punishes careless exploration. Read the threat before you commit: how an enemy approaches, which attacks carry the greatest risk, when to disengage, and which resources deserve to be spent.

| Threat Type | Threat Level | Attack Pattern | Key Weakness | Core Tactic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early-Act Hostiles | Moderate | Close-range pressure, predictable pursuit | Slow mobility, clear recovery windows | Use corners and doorways; keep distance |
| Fast Pursuers | High | Rapid closing, short attack chains | Struggles around obstacles and narrow routes | Break line of sight; attack only after they miss |
| Special Encounters | Severe | Waves plus hazards in limited space | Brief preparation window before escalation | Scout first; save the strongest gear for the final phase |

<Callout type="warning" title="Pursuer Rule">
Never retreat from a fast pursuer into unknown rooms. Move through previously cleared spaces where you already know the exits, and strike only during recovery animations.
</Callout>

For encounters that clearly signal high risk — unusual sound design, restricted movement, conspicuous empty space — complete nearby exploration first, enter with full healing capacity, and leave inventory room for rewards.

</Section>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Secrets, Puzzles, and Environmental Clues">

Optional content in Divine Frequency horror is woven into the environment rather than flagged on a map. Repeated symbols, unusual object placement, lighting changes, and areas that appear deliberately framed are all potential puzzle markers.

Work from the lightest spoiler outward:

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Observe unusual markings and repeated symbols | Identify what the environment is highlighting |
| 2 | Compare nearby rooms; note what repeats vs. what appears once | Isolate sequence markers from decoration |
| 3 | Revisit areas after story interactions change the environment | Newly accessible branches often hide rewards |
| 4 | Prepare fully before activating hidden encounters | Survive, then search the arena for lore items |

<Callout type="tip" title="Hidden Area Check">
The most valuable optional paths are often **adjacent to** the main route rather than directly inside it. Sweep side rooms and blocked sightlines before leaving any major exploration space.
</Callout>
