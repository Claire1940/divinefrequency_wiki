<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat Fundamentals for New Players">

Survival in Divine Frequency depends on reading encounters before committing. The game supports both firearms and psionic options, and the strongest players treat them as complementary tools rather than competing playstyles.

| Combat Tool | Combat Role | Main Resource | Upgrade Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Firearms** | Reliable sustained ranged damage | Ammunition | Damage consistency, accuracy, handling |
| **Psionic Options** | Crowd control, utility, burst damage | Psionic resources / ability availability | Effect strength, recovery, duration |
| **Weapon Mods** | Build specialization | Upgrade materials | Complementary bonuses, not stacked effects |
| **Hybrid Loadout** | Balanced encounter coverage | Shared ammo, healing, and upgrade budget | Reliability first, then small specializations |

<Callout type="tip" title="Ranged Discipline">
Do not rush into unfamiliar rooms. Check exits and nearby cover first, control the distance to enemies, and avoid spending every resource in the first exchange. Preserve high-impact psionic abilities for groups, dangerous targets, or blocked routes.
</Callout>

### Basic Engagement Rules

- Create distance before re-engaging after an enemy attack
- Use corners and doorways to control enemy approach paths
- Break line of sight against fast pursuers instead of fighting head-on
- Attack during recovery windows or after an enemy misses
- Keep one dependable damage option available at all times

</Section>

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Learn the combat rhythm">
    Test both firearm and psionic options early so you understand which tools work at which ranges. Use cover, control spacing, and avoid dumping resources into the first exchange of every fight.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Explore methodically and mark your route">
    Opening areas contain branching paths, locked doors, and optional encounters. Check side rooms before advancing a major objective, and remember which paths cannot be opened yet so you can return later.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Manage resources as one budget">
    Treat ammunition, healing, upgrade materials, and psionic resources as a single survival budget. Spend when an item solves a clear problem, not immediately after acquiring it. Keep a reserve for unavoidable encounters.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Build a simple progression plan">
    Choose one reliable damage approach first, then add complementary mods or abilities. Upgrades that make ordinary encounters more consistent are worth more early than narrow specializations.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Avoid common Early Access mistakes">
    Not every blocked path is a bug, and not every optional room must be cleared immediately. Separate inaccessible content from required progression, and revisit unresolved routes after major story advances.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Before Moving On">
Before leaving any area, confirm the main objective has advanced, important resources are collected, and blocked paths are recorded. This habit saves significant backtracking when Early Access content expands.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Builds and Resource Planning">

A good build makes ordinary encounters easier without leaving you helpless when conditions change. The three archetypes below reflect the game's main progression paths.

| Archetype | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Firearms-Focused** | Dependable ranged damage, simple resource planning | Ammunition dependence, weak when forced close | Players who like readable encounters and positioning |
| **Psionics-Focused** | Flexible control and utility, varied responses | Limited by ability recovery and timing demands | Players who enjoy active resource decisions |
| **Hybrid** | Handles changing ranges, reduces single-resource dependence | Slower to specialize, can waste upgrade materials | Players comfortable managing two systems |

### Build Planning Rules

- Prioritize **one reliable damage source**, one emergency option, and consistency upgrades
- Add a second damage role only after your primary setup handles ordinary encounters comfortably
- Choose mods that reinforce the same combat plan instead of stacking unrelated effects
- Revisit your setup when a new area introduces different combat demands
